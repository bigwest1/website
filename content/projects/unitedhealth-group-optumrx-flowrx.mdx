## Case Story Context

FlowRx required workflow clarity in a high-stakes operational environment where ambiguity creates errors and delay.

## Key UX Moves

- Simplified complex flow states into predictable user pathways.
- Reduced cognitive overload with cleaner prioritization and interaction hierarchy.
- Validated critical transitions with role-based scenario walkthroughs.

## Delivery Impact

Teams gained a more confident operational experience with stronger consistency and reduced navigation uncertainty.
