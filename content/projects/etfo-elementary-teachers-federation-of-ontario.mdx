## Case Story Context

ETFO needed a digital experience that could support high-volume information access while still feeling approachable to members with varied digital confidence levels.

## Key UX Moves

- Reframed navigation around real member tasks instead of org structure.
- Clarified content hierarchy to reduce scanning fatigue.
- Introduced interaction consistency patterns to increase trust and orientation.

## Delivery Impact

The resulting direction improved discoverability and gave implementation teams a clearer UX blueprint with lower ambiguity.
