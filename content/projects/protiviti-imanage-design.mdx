# iManage Design at Protiviti

## Context

Protiviti teams needed to find, evaluate, and act on internal knowledge artifacts quickly. The current experience made this harder than it should have been, especially when consultants were under delivery pressure.

## What I Led

- Mapped high-frequency consultant workflows from search intent to action.
- Reframed navigation around tasks instead of organizational silos.
- Reduced interaction friction in filter, sort, and drill-down behavior.
- Built and tested prototype rounds before implementation handoff.

## Design Shift

The redesign focused on fewer dead ends, clearer content signals, and faster orientation when users landed deep in the system. Instead of forcing users to learn the structure, the structure adapted to user intent.

## Outcome

The final direction improved internal findability confidence and created a more implementation-ready UX model for cross-functional teams.
