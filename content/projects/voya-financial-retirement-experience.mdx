# Voya Retirement Experience

## Context

Retirement planning is emotionally and financially high-stakes. The existing experience surfaced complexity too early, which made it harder for users to identify next steps with confidence.

## What I Solved For

- Clearer guidance moments at key decision points.
- Progressive disclosure for dense planning content.
- Better hierarchy for account actions and planning workflows.
- Interaction patterns that balance trust, clarity, and momentum.

## UX Approach

I translated business and compliance constraints into decision-friendly paths, then validated flows through prototype-based walkthroughs with stakeholders.

## Outcome

The updated direction made planning steps easier to understand and reduced ambiguity across core retirement workflows.
