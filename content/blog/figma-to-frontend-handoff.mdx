The handoff gap is usually not a tooling problem. It is a system problem: missing interaction intent, unclear states, and inconsistent component logic.

## Handoff Upgrade Pattern

1. Define interaction behavior, not just visuals.
2. Attach rationale to critical UX decisions.
3. Validate implementation with shared review checkpoints.

When design and engineering align on intent, velocity and quality both improve.
