Traditional personas decay quickly. AI-assisted clustering and synthesis can keep personas current and connected to evolving product behavior.

## Useful Persona System

- Source from real qualitative and quantitative inputs.
- Recompute segments on a regular cadence.
- Tie each persona directly to decision scenarios and design priorities.

When personas are treated as living artifacts, they become strategic tools instead of presentation slides.
