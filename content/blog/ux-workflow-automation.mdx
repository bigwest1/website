Automation should remove low-leverage repetition so teams can spend more time on strategy, synthesis, and design quality.

## Automate These First

- Research synthesis prep
- Handoff packaging
- QA checklists

## Keep Human-Led

- Problem framing
- Prioritization tradeoffs
- Experience quality judgments

Great UX organizations automate operations, not empathy.
