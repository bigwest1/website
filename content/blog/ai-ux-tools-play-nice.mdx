AI should amplify design judgment, not replace it. The strongest UX teams are pairing AI with research rigor to move faster and make sharper product decisions.

## Where AI Helps Most

- Rapid synthesis of interviews and usability notes.
- First-pass content and microcopy exploration.
- Pattern discovery across large behavioral data sets.

## Guardrails That Matter

1. Validate AI outputs with real user evidence.
2. Keep design intent and ethical decisions human-led.
3. Treat AI as a collaborator in the process, not the authority.

## Practical Stack

A practical flow is: **research ingestion -> AI summarization -> designer interpretation -> prototype validation**. When this loop is disciplined, teams gain speed without losing UX quality.
