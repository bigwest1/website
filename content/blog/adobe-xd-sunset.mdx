Adobe XD had strengths, but modern product teams now optimize for real-time collaboration, system governance, and rapid iteration.

## Why Teams Migrate

- Shared multiplayer workflows.
- Better component and token synchronization.
- Easier cross-functional handoff.

## Decision Lens

The best tool is the one that improves team throughput **and** quality. For most organizations, that means choosing ecosystems that connect design, content, engineering, and research.
