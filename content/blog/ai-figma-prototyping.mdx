AI can speed prototyping by generating variant ideas, interaction copy, and early-state explorations. The designer still curates, edits, and validates.

## Fast Loop

- Prompt for directional concepts.
- Convert to testable prototype paths.
- Validate with representative users.

Speed is only valuable when paired with clarity.
