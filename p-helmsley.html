Helmsley Charitable Trust

For the Helmsley Charitable Trust project, I led a comprehensive UX design process that began with discovery and requirements gathering. I facilitated in-depth workshops with stakeholders to align goals and identify user needs. These sessions informed the creation of detailed wireframes and interactive prototypes, which were iteratively refined based on user feedback reviews.

Throughout the project, I organized collaborative design sessions to ensure the team stayed aligned on vision and usability goals. Leveraging a mix of UX techniques, I delivered a design solution that streamlined workflows and improved the user experience, all while ensuring it met the specific requirements of the trust’s mission. This project highlighted my ability to drive user-centered design, foster collaboration, and deliver results that align with organizational goals.
