# Flagship Theme Park Course Brief

## Working Concept

A premium modern theme park golf course set inside a believable destination resort district. The course should feel authored by a world-class entertainment design team, not by a random collection of props.

## Creative Pillars

- Premium world detail with polished materials and intentional signage.
- Spectacle integrated into routing, not stapled onto holes.
- Distinct districts with their own atmosphere, landmarks, and audio-visual identity.
- Believable operations and guest-facing logic, even when the world is heightened.

## District Seeds

- Arrival marina and resort promenade.
- Futurist garden district.
- High-energy kinetic entertainment zone.
- Night-spectrum waterfront finale.

## Simulator Constraints

- Spectacle must not destroy shot readability.
- Landmark density must stay within performance budgets.
- Minimap and flyover outputs must remain clear despite world complexity.
