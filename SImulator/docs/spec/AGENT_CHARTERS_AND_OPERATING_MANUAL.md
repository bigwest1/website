# Agent Charters And Operating Manual

## Status

Approved for execution and now reflected in governance docs, the `agent-system` package, and the Agent Command Center screen foundation.

## Core Direction

- Treat the internal team as a disciplined product organization, not a loose set of assistants.
- Give `IRON FORGE` final internal authority for routine product, architecture, UX, sequencing, and engineering decisions.
- Keep ownership explicit across architecture, UX, simulator logic, worldbuilding, implementation, integrations, QA, reliability, preview, governance, and delivery management.
- Escalate to the human only for credentials, consent-sensitive purchases or licenses, material legal or IP exposure, or vision-breaking choices.
