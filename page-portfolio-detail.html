ETFO - Elementary Teachers' Federation of Ontario

I collaborated with the Elementary Teachers' Federation of Ontario (ETFO) to create an intuitive, responsive digital solution tailored to the needs of educators. This project encompassed the full UX design process, ensuring a user-centered approach at every stage.

Understanding the Needs

The process began with in-depth requirements gathering through in-person workshops with key stakeholders. These sessions provided valuable insights into the needs, goals, and pain points of ETFO's diverse user base. By engaging directly with educators and administrators, I was able to build a clear foundation for the project.

Shaping the Vision

With the gathered insights, I developed user personas to represent key user groups and conducted a competitive analysis to identify opportunities for differentiation. These findings informed the design strategy, ensuring the final product addressed specific user needs while maintaining a competitive edge.

Designing the Solution

The design phase involved creating wireframes, prototypes, and mockups using Axure and Photoshop. Starting with low-fidelity wireframes, I mapped out the structure and functionality of the solution. Rapid prototyping allowed for iterative feedback, ensuring that the design aligned with user expectations. High-fidelity mockups brought the concept to life, showcasing a polished visual design.

Bringing It to Life

After finalizing the design, I translated the prototypes into functional HTML, CSS, and jQuery, ensuring responsive design for optimal performance across devices. My expertise in front-end development allowed for seamless implementation, delivering a cohesive experience that met ETFO’s needs.

This project exemplifies the power of a comprehensive UX process, from collaborative workshops to hands-on coding, in delivering solutions that truly resonate with users.
